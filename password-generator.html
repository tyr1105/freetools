Frequently Asked Questions

Is this password generator safe?

Yes. Passwords are generated using crypto.getRandomValues() for true randomness. Nothing is sent to any server.

How long should my password be?

At least 12 characters for good security. 16+ characters for strong security. 20+ for maximum security.

Are generated passwords stored?

No. The recent history is only shown in your current session and cleared when you close the browser.
