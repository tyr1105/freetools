Frequently Asked Questions

Is this text to speech tool free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. No signup or credit card required.

Is my text data private?

Absolutely. Your text is processed entirely in your browser using the Web Speech API. Nothing is sent to any server.

Can I download the audio?

Yes! Click the "Download Audio" button to save the speech as a WAV audio file.

What voices are available?

The available voices depend on your browser and operating system. Most modern browsers provide multiple voices in different languages.
